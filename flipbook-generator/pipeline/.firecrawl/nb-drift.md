If that looks familiar, you are not alone. The same prompt, the same character, and yet a completely different person stares back at you every time. It is one of the most frustrating limitations of AI-generated imagery — and until recently, there was no clean solution.

Nano Banana 2 changes that. By the end of this guide you will have a reusable character sheet, a workflow for placing that character in any scene you can imagine, and a master prompt template you can copy directly. Your characters will finally look the same in every image, whether they are sitting in a Parisian café or walking through a rainy park.

Let’s fix it.

### What is Subject Consistency?

In the world of AI-generated art, **Subject Consistency** is the ability of a model to maintain the exact same identity, features, and proportions of a character or object across multiple different images.

Without this feature, an AI might generate a “writer with red hair” differently every time , changing her nose shape, the shade of her hair, or her age between shots. **Nano Banana 2** uses advanced “Identity Locking” to ensure that once you define a subject, they remain “them” whether they are sitting in a café, walking in a park, or appearing in a 4K technical diagram. For writers, this means you can finally “cast” a character for your story and have them look identical throughout an entire series of blog posts or chapters.
